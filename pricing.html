Questions

What's a credit?

One AI generation — a visualise, a pattern draft, a techpack render. Plans include a monthly pool; you can top up any time without changing tier.

Why per seat?

Studios work in teams. Seats share one credit pool, so you pay for people and pull compute from a common balance rather than rigid per-person caps.

How does production pricing work?

On Brand, you send a design to sampling or manufacture through our partners. You pay the run cost plus a 10–15% take-rate — nothing until you actually produce.

Can I start free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you the full canvas and 20 generations a month — no card. Upgrade when you need exports or more compute.
